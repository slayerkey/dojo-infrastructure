# 👋 Welcome to Slayerkey's Training Dojo!
*(Unfortunately also known as STD.)*

Glad you're here. This is a community for VALORANT players who want to improve with structure, not random grinding.

**You don't need to figure everything out. Follow your roadmap.**

## 🧭 START HERE — BEFORE YOUR FIRST WEEK
1. Read **#start-here** and watch the [Onboarding video](<https://whop.com/slayerkey/exp_gJq4d54kaCqWzC/app/courses/cors_EbCc3zaRKmonI/lessons/lesn_7emHFEKsx8iY4/>).
2. Go to **#🤖┃bots** and run `/roadmap`. This shows **your next task**, your progress, and **View Full 90 Days**.
3. Try `/roadmap-leaderboard` to see who's leading by completed roadmap tasks. Your ranked RR is tracked separately.

## ⏱️ YOUR FIRST HOUR
- Introduce yourself in **#👋┃introductions** (template pinned).
- Reply to **two other members**.
- Complete [Day 1 of the Fundamentals Sprint](<https://whop.com/slayerkey/exp_gJq4d54kaCqWzC/app/courses/cors_EbCc3zaRKmonI/lessons/lesn_rHCBrfAAys9m8/>).
- Submit your Day 1 task in **#🧪┃roadmap-tasks**.

## 📍 YOUR FIRST DAY
- Link your Riot account with `/linkriot` in **#🤖┃bots**.
- Post your goal in **#📅┃2026-goals**.
- Join a conversation in **#💬┃general**.
- Check the Events tab and mark yourself interested in an upcoming session.

## 📅 YOUR FIRST WEEK
- Complete **Days 2–7** of the Fundamentals Sprint and submit your tasks in **#🧪┃roadmap-tasks**.
- Post your first win in **#🏆┃wins**. A good game, a clip, a small improvement, or finishing the sprint all count.
- Run `/roadmap` again to check your next step.
- Check **#🤼┃premier-info** for Premier team opportunities.

## 🎯 MONTH 1 — FUNDAMENTALS + MECHANICS
Complete the [Month 1 training module](<https://whop.com/slayerkey/exp_gJq4d54kaCqWzC/app/courses/cors_S0QdgIpwwRmpJ>): aim mastery, crosshair placement, movement, and 2SS. Submit your **Mechanics Checkpoint** around Day 30.

## 🧠 MONTH 2 — AGENT + MAP MASTERY
Study pro gameplay for one agent and map at a time. Apply what you learn, build playbooks, and submit your playbook checkpoint around Day 60.

## 🔍 MONTH 3 — REVIEW + IMPROVE
Learn to review your own VODs, identify your biggest recurring mistake, and improve it each week. Finish the 90-day review checkpoint.

## 🤝 GET FEEDBACK + FIND YOUR TEAM
- **Weekly coaching / Open Office Hour:** Bring clips and questions. We meet in [Classroom VC](<https://discord.com/channels/1494446702378221590/1530226054210261032>). Check the Events tab for the next call.
- **#🎬┃clips / #🧑‍🏫┃community-help:** Share clips, ask questions, and help other players.
- **#🤼┃premier-info:** Apply for NA/EU Premier teams when spots open.
- **#🏆🏆┃big-wins:** Share the progress you're proud of.

**One task at a time. Run `/roadmap`. Do the work.**
